Grab Viewer

Load a Gaussian splat or 3D model, then grab the space around it to move, scale and turn it.

No model loaded.
Controls in VR
Grip (one hand)Grab space and push, pull or slide the model
Grip (both hands)Spread apart to zoom in, bring together to zoom out, turn like a steering wheel to rotate
A or XReset the model in front of you
B or YLeave VR and return to this page
TriggerPlay or pause the animation (animated models)
Thumbstick left / rightPrevious / next animation, if the model has several
Settings

Splats: .ply (3DGS), .spz, .splat, .ksplat, .sog, .zip (SOG), .rad
Models: .glb, .gltf (embedded), .obj, .fbx, .stl, .ply (mesh/points), .dae, .3mf, .usdz
