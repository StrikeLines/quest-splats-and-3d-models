Grab Viewer

Load a Gaussian splat or 3D model, then grab the space around it to move, scale and turn it.

Controls in VR
Grip (one hand)Grab space and push, pull or slide the model
Grip (both hands)Spread apart to zoom in, bring together to zoom out, turn like a steering wheel to rotate
A or XReset the model in front of you. Hold for 1 second to reset the pose.
B or YLeave VR and return to this page
Thumbstick pressPlay or pause the animation

Rigged models (posing):

Touch a joint + triggerTouch a joint (the dots near your controller) with the controller tip, hold the trigger and move your hand: forward/back, up/down, left/right. The joints above it bend to follow, and it stays pinned where you let go.
Both triggersGrab two joints at once, e.g. both shoulders: push one, pull the other to twist the torso
Trigger tap on a jointPin or unpin it without moving it
Thumbstick up / downMore / fewer joints bend when you drag
Thumbstick left / rightUndo / redo (on models that can't be posed: previous / next animation)

Mixamo rigs: joints stay within natural human ranges, knees and elbows bend the right way, and feet stay flat. To squat, pin both feet (tap them), then drag the hips down.
